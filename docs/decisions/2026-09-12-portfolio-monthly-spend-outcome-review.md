## Common evidence rules

This table is the populated first source sheet. Each row gives current source, actual cash as a value or unknown, actual owner time as a value or unknown, customer/outcome evidence, counterevidence, and a decision for Rex. It does not require a separate source sheet, dashboard, score, or scheduled job.

Unknown cash remains unknown, not zero. If Rex elects to compare effort, `logged hours × EUR75` is nominal only. It is not a receipt, saved cash, or substitute for customer outcome.

## Proposed relative review horizons

For a launched or tested case, collect its result from its actual launch or test start. At about three, six, and twelve months after that start, return evidence and a retain, change, pause, or stop recommendation to Rex. Each result, including pass, fail, and inconclusive, returns to Rex. It does not automatically expand, retire, restart, renew, or restrict work elsewhere.

Unlaunched initiatives have no fixed December failure date. This record does not choose a monthly review day. Rex can choose timing and acceptance thresholds when there is an actual initiative start or material allocation decision.

## Rex's choices

Rex chooses material allocation, scarce-resource commitments, cash thresholds, any SwimTrack release or money collection, and any editorial schedule change. The strategic decisions in this sheet remain recommendations.
